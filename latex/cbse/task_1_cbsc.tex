\textbf{General Instructions:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})}
    \item All questions are compulsory.
    \item The question paper consists of 31 questions divided into four sections - A, B, C and D.
    \item Section A contains 4 questions of 1 mark each, Section B contains 6 questions of 2 marks each, Section C contains 10 questions of 3 marks each, and Section D contains 11 questions of 4 marks each.
    \item Use of calculators is not permitted.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
{\fontsize{14pt}{16pt}\selectfont \textbf{SECTION - A}}\\[0.3cm]
\textbf{Question numbers 1 to 4 carry 1 mark each.}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]

\item In Fig.1, PQ is a tangent at point C to a circle with centre O. If AB is a diameter and $\angle CAB = 30^\circ$, find $\angle PCA$.

\item For what value of $k$ will $k+9$, $2k-1$ and $2k+7$ be consecutive terms of an A.P.?

\item A ladder leaning against a wall makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the horizontal. If the foot of the ladder is 2.5 m away from the wall, find the length of the ladder.

\item A card is drawn at random from a well-shuffled pack of 52 playing cards. Find the probability of getting neither a red card nor a queen.

\newpage

\begin{center}
{\fontsize{14pt}{16pt}\selectfont \textbf{SECTION - B}}\\[0.3cm]
\textbf{Question numbers 5 to 10 carry 2 marks each.}
\end{center}

\item If $-5$ is a root of the quadratic equation $2x^2 + px - 15 = 0$ and the quadratic equation $p(x^2+x)+k=0$ has equal roots, find the value of $k$.

\item Let P and Q be the points of trisection of the line segment joining A(2,-2) and B(-7,4), such that P is nearer to A. Find the coordinates of P and Q.

\item In Fig.2, a quadrilateral ABCD circumscribes a circle touching sides AB, BC, CD and DA at P, Q, R and S respectively. Prove that:
\[
AB + CD = BC + DA
\]

\item Prove that the points (3,0), (6,4), and (-1,3) are the vertices of a right-angled isosceles triangle.

\item The 4th term of an A.P. is zero. Prove that the 25th term is three times its 11th term.

\item In Fig.3, from an external point P, two tangents PT and PS are drawn to a circle with centre O and radius $r$. If $OP=2r$, show that:
\[
\angle OTS = \angle OST = 30^\circ
\]

\begin{center}
{\fontsize{14pt}{16pt}\selectfont \textbf{SECTION - C}}\\[0.3cm]
\textbf{Question numbers 11 to 20 carry 3 marks each.}
\end{center}

\item In Fig.4, O is the centre of a circle such that diameter AB = 13 cm and AC = 12 cm. Find the area of the shaded region. (Take $\pi = 3.14$)

\item In Fig.5, a tent is in the shape of a cylinder with a conical top of same diameter. If the cylindrical part has height 2.1 m and diameter 3 m, and slant height of cone is 2.8 m, find the cost of canvas needed at Rs. 500/sq.m. (Use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$)

\item If a point P(x,y) is equidistant from the points A(a+b, a-b), prove that:
\[
bx = ay
\]

\item In Fig.6, find the area of the shaded region enclosed between two concentric circles of radii 7 cm and 14 cm where $\angle AOC = 40^\circ$. (Use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$)

\item If the ratio of the sum of the first $n$ terms of two A.P.s is $(7n+1):(4n+27)$, find the ratio of their $m^{th}$ terms.

\item Solve:
\[
\frac{1}{(x-1)(x-2)}+\frac{1}{(x-2)(x-3)}=\frac{2}{3}, \quad x\neq1,2,3
\]
\item A conical vessel with base radius 5 cm and height 24 cm is full of water. This water is emptied into a cylindrical vessel of base radius 10 cm. Find the height to which water rises.

\item A sphere of diameter 12 cm is dropped into a cylindrical vessel partially filled with water. If the water rises by $3\frac{5}{9}$ cm, find the diameter of the vessel.

\item A man standing on the deck of a ship 10 m above water level observes the angle of elevation of the top of a hill as $60^\circ$ and angle of depression of the base as $30^\circ$. Find the distance of the hill from the ship and the height of the hill.

\item Three different coins are tossed together. Find the probability of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item exactly two heads
\item at least two heads
\item at least two tails
\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
{\fontsize{14pt}{16pt}\selectfont \textbf{SECTION - D}}\\[0.3cm]
\textbf{Question numbers 21 to 31 carry 4 marks each.}
\end{center}

\item Due to heavy floods, 50 schools collectively offered to provide place and canvas for 1500 tents. The lower part of each tent is cylindrical of radius 2.8 m and height 3.5 m, with a conical top of height 2.1 m. If canvas costs Rs.120 per sq.m, find the amount shared by each school. (Use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$)

\item Prove that the lengths of two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

\item Draw a circle of radius 4 cm. Draw two tangents inclined at an angle of $60^\circ$ to each other.

\item In Fig.7, two equal circles with centres O and O' touch each other. Find:
\[
\frac{DO'}{CO}
\]

\item Solve:
\[
\frac{1}{x+1}+\frac{2}{x+2}+\frac{4}{x+4}=\frac{2}{3}, \quad x\neq -1,-2,-4
\]

\item The angle of elevation of the top Q of a vertical tower PQ from point X on the ground is $60^\circ$. From a point Y, 40 m vertically above X, the angle of elevation is $45^\circ$. Find the height of the tower and distance OX. (Use $\sqrt{3}=1.73$)

\item The houses in a row are numbered consecutively from 1 to 49. Show that there exists a value of X such that the sum of house numbers preceding X equals the sum of numbers following X.

\item In Fig.8, the vertices of $\triangle ABC$ are A(4,6), B(1,5), and C(7,2). A line segment DE intersects AB and AC such that:
\[
\frac{AD}{AB}=\frac{AE}{AC}=\frac{1}{3}
\]

\item A number x is selected at random from \{1,2,3,4\} and another number y from \{1,4,9,16\}. Find the probability that $xy < 16$.

\item In Fig.9, a sector OAP of a circle with centre O contains angle $\theta$. AB is perpendicular to OA and meets OP produced at B. Prove that the perimeter of the shaded region is:
\[
r\left[\tan\theta+\sec\theta+\frac{\pi\theta}{180}-1\right]
\]

\item A motor boat whose speed is 24 km/h in still water takes 1 hour more to go 32 km upstream than to return downstream. Find the speed of the stream.

\end{enumerate}
